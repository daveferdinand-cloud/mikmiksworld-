# Mika’s World: creative direction

## Brief

The original site looked attractive but most rooms offered too little useful activity. The new product is for a capable tween with creative interests. It should invite sustained making and personal expression, without talking down to the person using it. Mika’s own interests remain the organising principle: art, crafts, building, stories, recipes, favourite music, books, plushes, and her cousins.

The approved existing illustrations remain central. Her exact three favourite songs remain Enchanted, Blank Space, and Love Story. Approval concerned the music option, not its previous layout. Do not reintroduce the boxed music panel or nine-card dashboard as the organising structure.

## Visual language

Warm paper, cherry/plum ink, quiet sage and lavender, large expressive serif typography, small precise navigation, and handwritten marginal notes. The homepage unfolds through alternating illustrated spreads, irregular rounded image edges, open space, numbered sections, and purposeful text links. Tools have controls where controls are useful; the site itself should feel like a world and a working notebook rather than an enterprise dashboard.

The home introduces the creative identity and gives a clear starting action. Music is a slim selection ribbon on home and a visible, optional persistent player after a click. Creative rooms have their own context, but share a coherent navigation and notebook.

## Product principles

1. Every room must allow useful action, not simply describe something.
2. Teach techniques that transfer: perspective, expression, fabric, composition, story structure, and making processes.
3. Support open-ended work. Prompts are invitations, not the full extent of the tool.
4. Preserve work. Drafts, drawing strokes, favourites, notes, collections, checklists, and finished pieces persist locally.
5. Rewards reflect real work. Opening a room or clicking a completion button without finishing a checklist is not a creative achievement.
6. Personalisation comes through approved illustrated identity, interests, favourite music, and creative ownership. No identifying private family or school details.
7. No accounts, personal-data services, public uploads, or social features.

## Deliberate scope

No generative runtime API, external chatbot, online sharing, recipe nutrition claims, licensed audio downloads, or fabricated skill scoring. Art instruction uses deterministic original diagrams. Music is the existing credited embedded material. Stories are stored privately in the local bookshelf; “bind my book” does not publish to the public web.

The two original craft destinations are preserved as one deeper making table with a searchable/filterable project library, a separate idea-finder mode, and persistent project progress. The story room and bookshelf form a complete creation-to-collection journey. The plush room now supports character creation and connects back to making and writing.
